Where products lose people

Onboarding that never reaches value

People sign up, land in an empty product, and never reach the moment that proves it works. We design the shortest honest path to first value, and the empty states that carry users there.

Core flows that grew by accident

The flow that worked for one use case now serves four, with branches nobody designed. We map it as it is, then redraw it around the job the user actually came to do.

A system drifting out of sync

Buttons in sizes nobody agreed on, colours that shift between screens, engineers rebuilding the same component. We tokenize what exists and document what ships.

Three steps. No surprises.

Audit and align

We review the product as it is, read your analytics, and talk to your team about where users stall. You get a written audit with the problems ranked by revenue impact, not by how easy they are to fix.

Flows, then interface

We redraw the core journeys and sign them off with your engineers before any pixel is placed. Then interface design, states, and micro-interactions, delivered in reviewable batches every 48 hours.

Handoff that survives the sprint

Components, tokens, and annotations your developers can build from without a meeting. We stay available through implementation so the shipped product matches the designed one.

Questions on product design

What does a product design agency do?

A product design agency designs how software works and looks: user flows, interface design, states, and the design system that keeps it consistent. Radiant works as an embedded partner — we join your product process rather than delivering a file and leaving.

How long does a product design project take?

An audit takes two weeks. A core flow redesign takes four to six. A full product design engagement typically runs eight to twelve weeks from kickoff to handoff, with work in your hands every 48 hours throughout.

Working with your team

Can you work with our existing design system?

Yes. We audit what you have, work inside it, and tell you plainly where it is costing you speed. Rebuilding a system is a separate decision we will not make for you mid-project.

Do you work directly with our engineers?

Yes, and the work is better when we do. We sign flows off with engineering before visual design starts, so what we hand over is buildable against your real data and constraints.
