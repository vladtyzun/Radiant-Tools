Why decks fail in the room

The story arrives in the wrong order

Investors need the problem, the insight, and the evidence before the product tour. We restructure the narrative first; design cannot rescue a sequence that argues backwards.

The numbers are decorated, not explained

Three-dimensional pie charts and unlabelled axes read as hiding something. We design each chart around the one comparison it exists to make.

Every slide competes for attention

If everything is bold, nothing is. One idea per slide, a visual hierarchy that survives a projector, and a version that still works when read on a phone.
