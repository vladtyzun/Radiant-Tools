What we fix in fintech products

KYC that people abandon

Document upload is where funded fintechs quietly lose their cohort. We design onboarding that explains why each step exists, saves progress, and shows how much is left — then instrument it so you can see where the drop really happens.

Trust signals arriving too late

Licensing lines, partner banks, and risk disclosures pasted into the footer at the end of a build. We plan them from the first wireframe, so they read clearly and appear where the user is deciding.

A brand indistinguishable from the category

Blue gradients, floating cards, the same three icons. We build identities that feel as safe as a bank and look like nobody else — tested against your product UI, not just a logo sheet.

Fintech design questions

How do fintech websites build trust?

By answering the safety question before the feature list: who holds the money, which licence or partner bank applies, what happens if something goes wrong. Those belong above the fold, not in the footer.

How do you design KYC people actually finish?

Explain the reason for each step, ask for the hardest document when motivation is highest, save progress, and show remaining steps. Then measure each stage separately — the average completion rate hides where the loss is.

Do you handle compliance sign-off?

No, and be wary of a studio that says it does. We design disclosures, consent, and risk wording to be clear and prominent, then your counsel approves the final language.

Working with fintech teams

Do you sign NDAs and work in stealth?

Yes, as standard. A good share of our fintech work has started before public launch, and we treat funding timing as confidential until you announce it.

Can you design both the product and the marketing site?

Yes, and in fintech it matters more than elsewhere: the credibility built on the site has to survive the first product screen. One team keeps that promise intact.
