What we fix in SaaS products

An empty product on day one

New accounts land in a blank dashboard that proves nothing. We design the shortest honest route to first value — sample data, guided setup, and empty states that teach rather than apologise.

One interface, four roles

The admin, the daily user, the approver, and the person who only visits monthly all share one navigation. We separate the jobs so nobody wades through someone else's workflow.

A website written for analysts

Feature tables convince nobody on their own. We structure the site around the buying committee — the user's job, the manager's business case, and security's checklist each get a clear route.

SaaS design questions

How do you improve activation?

Define the activation event honestly, measure every step to it separately, then remove or defer everything between signup and that moment. Most gains come from deletion, not addition.

How long does a SaaS product redesign take?

A single flow takes four to six weeks. A full product redesign runs eight to twelve, usually phased by surface so you ship improvements while the rest is still in design.

Can you design for a product we are still building?

Yes. Designing alongside engineering is normal for us — we sign flows off with your developers against real data constraints before visual design starts.

Working with product teams

Do you work inside our sprints?

Yes. On retainer we join your rituals, take tickets, and deliver in your cadence. On project work we run our own and sync at agreed checkpoints.

Can you work with our PM and engineers?

That is the arrangement that works best. Engineering sees the flows before they are visually designed, which is what keeps handoff from becoming renegotiation.
